Cyber mode on your own game

In G4 the suite turns on the game it built: Terrain draws the game's servers, functions and keys as terrain, and Forge replays forged entitlements, replayed device challenges and packet floods against a staging copy. Until Forge has a real isolation backend, drills run only against our own clients.
